Gasless & relayer

Owners and customers never pay network fees. They sign typed data; the relayer submits; the contract only moves value to exactly what was signed.

registerFor

Owner registers a shop. Signature binds merchant = signer.

payFor + EIP-2612 permit

Customer pays. Permit authorises exactly the signed amount to this shop.

acceptLoanFor

Owner accepts an offer. Terms are re-read from chain, not from the payload.

Attack cases

AttackWhat the relayer triesResult
TamperChange amount after signingBadSignature
RedirectSwap the merchant addressBadSignature
ReplayResubmit a used signatureBadSignature

When the free relayer is busy, the app falls back to a normal wallet transaction and the user pays gas themselves (owner app copy: "Layanan gratis sedang ramai").
